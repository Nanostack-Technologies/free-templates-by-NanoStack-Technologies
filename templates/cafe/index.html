About Us

At Brew & Bite, we believe in slow mornings, strong coffee, and sweet moments. Founded in 2020, our café blends the aroma of premium beans with the warmth of home-style baking.

100% Arabica Beans

Freshly Baked Pastries

Free Wi-Fi & Cozy Interiors

Work or relax in our comfortable space with high-speed internet and cozy seating.
